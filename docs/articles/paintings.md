# The paintings

Each palette is drawn from one or more works by Candido Portinari
(1903–1962). Eight are in the collection of the [Museu de Arte de São
Paulo (MASP)](https://masp.org.br/en/collections); *Guerra* and *Paz*
are the two panels he painted for the United Nations Headquarters in New
York. Click an image or title to open the work’s page.

The palettes are an interpretation of each work’s dominant tones, not a
measurement of it. Images are shown from the MASP collection site and
remain under copyright.
